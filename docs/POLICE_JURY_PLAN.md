# Parks, Recreation and Trails as a Division of the East Carroll Parish Police Jury

Prepared 2026-10-29. This is the political and organizational plan. `FUNDING_ROADMAP.md` and `TRAILS_PLAN.md` hold the program detail.

## 1. Why a Police Jury division rather than a separate district

The Police Jury already has the legal standing, the SAM.gov history (it receives federal awards and files a Single Audit), the road crews, and the equipment. Making parks and trails a division of the Jury means:

- Grant applications go out under the Jury's existing registrations, so nothing waits on a new entity.
- The Road Maintenance and Construction fund's crews and equipment can build trail base, clear corridors, and maintain trailheads on a cost-share basis that counts as local match.
- Road and trail money can be pursued together. Most of the trail programs (TAP, LRSP, Safe Routes) are transportation money that a road division already understands.
- A recreation district under La. R.S. 33:4562 can still be created later if the Jury wants a dedicated millage, and the division's track record makes that vote easier.

## 2. Proposed structure

```
East Carroll Parish Police Jury
└── Parks, Recreation & Trails Division
    ├── Division coordinator (half-time to start; grant-funded by year 2)
    ├── GIS and grants support (shared with Road Department and E911)
    ├── Road Department (existing) provides crews for construction and maintenance
    └── Advisory committee: one appointee per juror district, plus
        Town of Lake Providence, School Board, LDWF (Bayou Macon WMA), Port Commission
```

**Jury actions needed, in order:**

1. Resolution creating the division and naming a juror as liaison (one meeting).
2. Resolution authorizing the president to sign grant applications and designating the secretary-treasurer as financial contact for federal awards.
3. Resolution adopting the parks and trails master plan once NPS RTCA helps draft it (spring 2027).
4. Annual budget line for the division, even if small, because every state program asks for evidence of local commitment.

## 4. The pitch, in one page

- East Carroll has 7,195 residents, a 35.7 percent poverty rate, and no recreation fund, no built trail, and no recreation district.
- The parish already owns the raw material: 48 km of levee road, a 55 km dormant rail corridor, Lake Providence frontage, ball fields, two boat ramps, and Bayou Macon WMA on its border.
- Every census tract qualifies as low-to-moderate income, the parish is a USDA persistent-poverty county, and it is a DRA distressed parish. That is the highest funding tier in nearly every program listed below.
- Year one costs the Jury a resolution, a liaison juror, and staff time. Year two brings the first construction dollars (TAP, RTP, LWCF).
- The same registrations and GIS work unlock road and bridge money the Jury is leaving on the table.

## 5. Road and bridge funding the Jury can pursue alongside parks

| Program | Agency | What it pays for | Share | Deadline / cycle | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Parish Transportation Fund** | LA Treasury via DOTD | Parish road and bridge maintenance; formula allocation | 100% state | Annual, automatic | $46.4 million statewide in FY 2026-27. The Jury must adopt a Parish Road Program and system of administration each year to receive it. Trail segments on parish road rights-of-way can be built with it. |
| **Off-System Bridge Program** | LA DOTD | Replacement or rehab of structurally deficient parish bridges | 80% federal / 20% state, no local match in most cases | Annual compliance resolution; programming cycles | Jury adopts a yearly certification resolution. Unselected allotments can be banked. Louisiana expects about $1 billion in federal Bridge Formula money over five years, part of it for off-system bridges. |
| **Local Road Safety Program** | LA DOTD / LTAP | Safety improvements on parish-owned roads: signing, striping, curve warnings, shoulders, intersection fixes | 100% federal, up to $500,000 per project | Year-round or quarterly cutoffs (Mar 31, Jun 30, Sep 30, Dec 31); confirm | Projects tied to a parish Local Road Safety Plan score higher. Pedestrian and bike safety counts. Contact LCTS 225-767-9718. |
| **Transportation Alternatives Program** | LA DOTD | Sidewalks, shared-use paths, Safe Routes to School, streetscape | 80% federal | Next call expected early 2027 | The bridge between roads and trails. $20 million statewide per call. |
| **LCDBG Public Facilities, streets category** | LA Office of Community Development | Street and drainage improvements in LMI areas | ~0-10% local | Two-year cycle; FY 2028 info August 2027 | One application per cycle, so choose between streets and a recreation facility, or pair streets with the trail. |
| **DRA SEDAP, transportation category** | Delta Regional Authority | Roads, bridges, and multimodal access that support economic development | Distressed parish: low match | NOFA ~June 2027 | Apply through North Delta RPDD. |
| **USDOT BUILD** | US DOT | Larger road, bridge, and multimodal projects | Up to 100% federal for rural | FY 2026 closed Feb 24, 2026; watch for FY 2027 NOFO | Rural minimum request $1 million. Realistic only for a bundled corridor project (levee road plus trail plus port access). |
| **Safe Streets and Roads for All** | US DOT | Planning grants for a parish safety action plan; implementation grants after | 80% federal | FY 2026 closed May 26, 2026; next NOFO expected spring 2027 | Start with a planning grant (often $100,000 to $250,000). The action plan then qualifies the parish for LRSP and implementation money. |
| **Rural Surface Transportation Grant** | US DOT (MPDG) | Rural road and bridge projects | 80% federal | FY 2026 was the final authorized year; watch for successor | Large projects only. |
| **Louisiana Capital Outlay** | LA Legislature | Road, bridge, drainage, and facility projects | State bonds | Requests due November 1 | Needs a legislator sponsor. |
| **FEMA Public Assistance and Hazard Mitigation** | GOHSEP | Road and bridge repair after declared disasters; mitigation of repeat-damage roads | 75% federal | After declarations | The Jury's road crews already know this path. |
| **USDA Rural Development Community Facilities** | USDA RD | Road equipment, graders, trucks for a public body | Up to 75% grant in persistent-poverty counties | Rolling | Equipment that serves both roads and trails. |

**First three road actions:** adopt the Off-System Bridge compliance resolution if it has lapsed, ask LTAP for a Local Road Safety Plan (they help write it at no cost), and put the parish on the SS4A planning-grant list for the next NOFO.

1. **Pre-meeting, November 2026.** Meet the president and secretary-treasurer individually. Leave the one-pager and the map.
