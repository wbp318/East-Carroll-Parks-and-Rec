Online Records

Records held by the East Carroll Parish Clerk of Court can be searched remotely through eClerksLA, the statewide online access system used by Louisiana clerks of court.

eClerksLA

eClerksLA requires an account. Subscriptions, passwords, and billing are handled through eClerksLA. Please read the terms of use before you subscribe.

In person

Records may also be searched at the Clerk of Court’s office during business hours. Copy and certification fees are listed on the Fees page.
