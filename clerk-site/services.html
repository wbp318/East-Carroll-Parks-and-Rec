Marriage licenses

Both parties must appear in person. Please be at the office no later than 4:00 p.m. The license costs $32.50, cash only. No checks are accepted.

What to bring

  • Birth certificates and a picture ID for both parties. A foreign birth certificate must come with an appropriate translation.
  • If either party was previously married: a copy of each divorce decree, the date and place of each divorce, and whether each former spouse is living or deceased.

Rules to know

  • You must wait 30 days after a final divorce to apply for a license.
  • You must wait 24 hours after receiving the license to be married.
  • The license is good for 30 days, in Louisiana only.
  • Applicants under 18: parental consent and other requirements apply under Louisiana law. Please call the office before you come.
  • Any exception must be authorized by the district judge.

Birth and death certificates

To get a certified copy of a birth or death certificate, bring one primary document or two secondary documents. Fees are on the Fees page.

Primary documents (bring one)

  • Current state-issued driver’s license with a photograph that clearly identifies you
  • Current state-issued picture identification
  • Current U.S. military identification card with photograph
  • Current U.S. or foreign passport
  • U.S. Certificate of Naturalization (Form N-550, N-570, or N-578)
  • U.S. Certificate of Citizenship (Form N-560 or N-578)
  • Students in high school or younger: a current school yearbook or school ID with a photograph

Secondary documents (bring two)

  • Current college or university student picture ID with a paid-in-full fee receipt for the current semester (counts as two)
  • A W-2 issued within the last two years plus your original signed Social Security card, with matching numbers (counts as two)
  • Original adoption papers
  • Official certified deed or title to property
  • Certificate of vehicle title
  • Insurance policy showing your name (health, home, life, or auto)
  • Payroll stub, not handwritten, showing your name and Social Security number
  • Current U.S. military dependent ID card with photograph
  • Original DD-214 military discharge document
  • Certified true copy of your voter registration application
Important: if an applicant provides or attests to false information, the person who signs the application is the one prosecuted.

Recording a document

Any document filed for recordation must:

  • Be captioned as to the type of act on the first page
  • Have a 2-inch margin at the top of the first page
  • Have 1-inch margins at the bottom and sides of every page
  • Use type no smaller than 8 point

Documents that do not comply are charged a non-compliance fee. Recording fees are on the Fees page.

Elections

The Clerk of Court is the chief election officer of the parish. Voter registration is handled by the East Carroll Parish Registrar of Voters.

Use the Louisiana Secretary of State’s voter portal to check your registration, find your polling place, see a sample ballot, and view results.
